## Design

**Approved design:** Accepted 2026-10-27 at `109fd8df85783557ea0efc4bba0ada53355f5c74`; [canonical slice][design]; [actual review](https://github.com/aws-e/adp/pull/9106#pullrequestreview-5669812097).

**Reuse:** Shared L5 SQL/workers, Product APIs/broker and contracts; canonical audit pins main/open-PR evidence. WA entry points are proposed.

**Contract:** Integrate contracts/schema/retention, scoped broker/provider roles, private SQS worker factory and recovery, gateway/front-end/CLI/MCP and consumer publication in compatible batches. Retain normal gateway/frontend auto-deploys and one coordinated L5 worker/infra release per batch. Additive migrations preserve old service rows; rollback disables admissions but retains unknown native work and recoverable results. Verify exact installed artifacts and two non-ADP architectures/goals without code edits or redeployment. Own integration fixes and EC-to-AC evidence closure.

**Owned/advisory files:** L5 package, migrations, worker infra/live tests and service runbook; host/build/workflow inputs with #8698/#8932. Full advisory file groups in canonical slice.

**Dependencies:** #9098, #9099, #9100, #9101, #9102, #9104. **Prerequisites:** WA07 supplies eligible private fixtures and exact selected-role access within existing budgets; PranavSharma1000 coordinates. #8698/#8932 release and WA05 consumer contracts gate dependent live checks only. Filing grants no deployment, purchase or IAM/budget expansion.
