## The problem in plain terms

Operators need a reliable way to attach optional MLflow tracking to training results. The current implementation does not yet provide this outcome under the four-layer factory contract.

**The fix in one line:** Attach optional MLflow tracking to training results.

## Description

- **Parent / reports to:** https://github.com/aws-e/adp/issues/8950.
- **Delivery owner:** ADP delivery-flow coordinator assigns the SPF-T13 implementer; the coordinator owns integration and finding closure.
- **Size:** Medium. **Workstream:** tracking. **Release batch:** R2.
- **Completion boundary:** Merged implementation and its relevant tests; live deployment/acceptance is owned by batch R2 release/verification stories.
- **In scope:** Add an optional tracking adapter with explicit placement, tenant/storage ownership and scoped artifact links. Correlate experiments/runs/metrics/model artifacts with durable Superplane runs. Basic status/output verification must work with tracking disabled or unavailable.
- **Out of scope:** Other factory/provider/application outcomes owned by sibling stories; current workspace recovery remains under #6970.
