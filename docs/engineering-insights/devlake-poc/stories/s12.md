## Interfaces, data and authorization

Input: authenticated actor/consumer/workload, existing binding and selected source/purpose, operation/attempt and current policy refs. Output: opaque versioned context and server-resolved route with tenant/Project/realm/destination/audience/expiry/generations. Realm is a versioned sharing-policy expression, not a frozen user list. Nested source/evidence policies conjoin; unknown/private data cannot route to shared raw or diagnostics. SQL/S3 provisioning remains S02, not authority resolver side effect.

### Retained implementation requirements

- **Deliverables and entry points:** Proposed contracts/connector_scope schemas and gateway-owned cell/policy resolver APIs; Go/Insights use explicit interfaces. Pin request/response/error schemas before consumers integrate.
- **Inputs and configuration:** Authenticated tenant/workload and effective actor; registered Project/connection; purpose, source selection, operation/attempt, expiry and binding/grant revisions. Resolve cell=(tenant,Project,policy_realm) and destinations server-side; body IDs grant no authority.
- **Outputs and behavior:** Versioned scoped execution context plus policy dependencies. Realm describes compatible source-reader AND execution-evidence policies, not cached membership. Distinct policies use separate cells; unknown classification is rejected or assigned an authorized restricted destination before persistence.
- **Compatibility and data:** Canonical six-field record IDs stay unchanged. Cell/realm copies cannot multiply facts/cost. Validate nested dependencies; a producer cannot self-declare shared visibility. Gateway owns migrations/authority; no Go/Insights gateway-table reads.

The canonical architecture resolves placement, policy, publication and lifecycle details when the older implementation-contract summary is less specific. It does not relax any acceptance predicate. Common six-field identities, native provenance and current authorization remain mandatory.
