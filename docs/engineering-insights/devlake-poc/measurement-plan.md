# S01 versioned measurement plan

Revision 1, proposed for acceptance-owner review under [#8888](https://github.com/aws-e/adp/issues/8888). The [pilot contract](pilot-contract.json) is the source for numeric ceilings; the [field map](source-field-map.json) supplies candidate native provenance. **No controlled update, source timestamp, live metric, budget approval or cost observation has been recorded.** Approval and deployment are separate from these definitions. Any changed oracle, sample, ceiling or map requires a new version and independent review before using earlier measurements.

## Admission and controlled inputs

Select one registered primary Product/Project, two authorized tools (GitHub and a newly supported provider, candidate Jira), two connections, at most three primary scopes and a 30-day history; record connection/grant, policy realm, actual provider auth mode, exclusions and mapping revision privately. Source and acceptance owners jointly select **20 distinct controlled native updates** distributed across **at least 48 hours** from earliest to latest independently recorded source-event timestamp (not from collector start to report generation). Record intended update ordinal, provider, native namespace/ID, exact pre/post revision or observation-only digest, source event UTC time, native record/relationship oracle, allowed reader and policy in the private manifest before computing results. Include both providers; pin an observation clock and maximum clock-skew tolerance with the acceptance owner before collection. Without independently defensible source event times, source-to-result freshness is **unavailable**, never estimated from ingestion time. Never publish private identifiers or objects.

At most two collections run concurrently across distinct cells. Interactive Profile discovery is limited to 100 items/page, five pages and 30 seconds/operation; a partial page after the cap is partial coverage, not a complete source scan. No source call is made without current authorization. An authorized update that falls outside observed scope/history, is filtered by an approved policy, fails collection, or cannot be reconciled still occupies one of the 20 expected-update slots with an explicit outcome. Do not replace a slow, missing or private update with an easier update after the observation begins. Synthetic timing fixtures exercise the formulas but cannot satisfy the 48-hour native campaign.

Native issue status/type mappings retain original values and field-map version; unsupported fields are unavailable rather than filled from title/labels. Reconcile intended update IDs -> independent native record/relationship digest -> one sealed generation -> acknowledged immutable admission -> qualified dataset/Profile revision -> authorized API/CLI readback; assert no duplicates, silent omissions, cross-tool title merges or policy widening. A rejected private/foreign update must not alter shared count, lag denominator, coverage or pointer. For a source revision without provider version use a clearly labeled observation-only digest: it does not prove historical completeness.

## Proposed ceilings and stop conditions

These are *maximums for planning*, not proof of existing capacity or approved budget. Pilot: 14-day expiry, 30-day lookback, two connections, three primary scopes, two concurrent jobs, 100,000 sealed staging rows and 100 MiB sealed payload/link bytes **per cell/batch**. Proposed full-mode job allocation: at most 2 vCPU and 4 GiB memory **per job**; 10 GiB of incremental cell storage **per cell**, inclusive of staging and retained copies. Proposed total incremental pilot spend <=USD 100 including optional compute, storage, transfer, provider charges and retained storage through authorized cleanup; this is not a zero-cost claim. Four separately budgeted adversarial cells do not enlarge the primary connection/scope/concurrency limits. The DB/operator and acceptance owners must approve or explicitly lower these caps after measuring instance headroom, rate quotas, billing coverage and expiry/cleanup cost **before** any collection or resource provisioning. If they cannot bound a charge, prevent the dependent work, not reinterpret unknown as within USD 100. Stop admitting work before any limit or source/API quota is exceeded; retain already sealed evidence and trigger owned finalization on expiry/failure. Do not provision a validation environment to test this plan.

## Evidence and decision

The private run record carries pinned contract/source/code/image/schema/map/policy revisions, the 20 update oracles, approved skew/cutoff, intended event times, native response/hash and source-reader authorities, timestamps at each boundary, actual numerator/denominator, 48-hour span, p95 and threshold, independent count/hash/relationship comparison, per-resource peak and cost ledger (including retained costs), limits/stop receipts, exclusion/denial reasons, every attempt and cleanup/hold checks. Record metric status as pass/fail/blocked/unavailable/not-run and identify a separate acceptance reviewer. A synthetic pass is only a formula check; without source-owner observations or billing inputs the live metric stays unavailable. AC-03 freezes the rules before measurement; acceptance owner, not a document checker, approves them and any actual campaign result.
