## Findings (by confidence)

### HIGH — merge blockers
None.

### MEDIUM — discuss before merge
- `invoke_stream()` (line 203-213) and the 3 `_stream_*_response` methods use `await` in `finally` blocks of async generators. This works correctly when the generator is fully consumed or properly closed via `aclose()` (which FastAPI/Starlette does). Edge case: if client disconnects and generator is GC'd without `aclose()`, the log row may be lost. This is acceptable per the issue design ("a row may be lost if the pod is killed mid-write").

### LOW — follow-up candidates
- `cost_usd=0.0` is hardcoded in all new logging calls. The original `invoke()` computed cost from `self._cost_calculator`. Future follow-up could plumb cost calculation into the inner helpers.
- `_invoke_bedrock_response` and `_stream_bedrock_response` log `model=bedrock_model_id` (the resolved Bedrock ID) while the OpenAI/Anthropic variants log the original user-facing `model` name. Minor inconsistency but matches what the routes pass in.
